# CADENCE for members

Your gym's app: your workouts, your progress, your trainer, your check-ins.

## Start (3 minutes)
1. Open the address your gym gave you, then **Create new profile**. Your phone saves a passkey:
   you sign in with your fingerprint or face, no password.
2. Add it to your home screen: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**, or Chrome → menu →
   **Install app**.
3. **Your trainer**: Settings → **My trainer** → enter their code (for example `AMAN01`) → read
   what they will see → **Share**.

## At the gym
- **Check in**: Home → **Check in at the desk** → scan the code on the screen at reception (or
  just point your phone camera at it). One visit counts every 3 hours.
- **Train**: Home → **Start**. Your trainer's routines are marked "From Aman".

## Your PT sessions
Settings → My trainer shows sessions used and left, and each session your trainer counted. If one
wasn't you, tap **Not me** within 48 hours: the front desk checks it.

## Your data
Your trainer sees your workouts, body weight, measurements and routine names, never your photos,
videos or notes. Stop sharing any time: Settings → My trainer → **Stop sharing**. Delete your
profile and everything in it: Settings → **Delete account**. `PRIVACY.md` explains everything.
