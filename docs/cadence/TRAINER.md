# CADENCE for trainers

Everything about your clients in one place: what you assigned, what they did, their PT sessions,
diet chart and your notes.

## Start (5 minutes)
1. Create your profile at the gym's address. The owner makes you a trainer.
2. Your code is on **Home → Your clients** (for example `AMAN01`). Give it to your clients: they
   enter it under **Settings → My trainer** and agree to share their training with you.

## Each client: Home → Your clients → tap a name
- **Alerts**: missed 2 planned sessions in a row, a stalled lift (3 sessions without beating its
  best), a new PR this week.
- **PT sessions**: **Session done** after you train them (today in one tap, **Other day…** for a
  session you forgot). The client gets a notification. **Undo** works for 24 hours. **Add next
  package** when they renew.
- **Programme**: build routines in your own Plan, then **Assign programme**: the routines and
  the days land in the client's app. Below it: this month's consistency, sets done, on target.
- **Diet chart**: calories, protein, water and the meals. **Copy from another client** to start
  from one you already wrote. The client reads it in My trainer.
- **Your notes**: private to you (not the client, not the owner). They are deleted if the client
  stops sharing with you.
- **Workouts**: each workout, exercise by exercise, against what you asked for.

## The client list
Clients who need you come first: no workout for 7 days, missed sessions, a stalled lift, or a PT
package that is running out, expiring, flagged or just finished (time to sell the next one).
