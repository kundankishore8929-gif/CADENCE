# The CADENCE pilot: one gym, 2 weeks

**Goal:** find out whether a real gym (1 owner, 2 trainers, about 20 members) uses CADENCE every
day without help, and what gets in the way.

## Before day 1
1. Server running at the gym's address (`deploy/pilot/README.md`), backups on.
2. Owner profile, gym name and logo, 2 trainers made, desk screen at reception (`OWNER.md`).
3. Trainers have read `TRAINER.md`; members get `MEMBER.md` and `PRIVACY.md`.

## Week 1: get everyone in
- Day 1: trainers sign up their clients at the gym, face to face (5 minutes each).
- Days 1–3: every client checks in at the desk on each visit; trainers count PT sessions in the
  app instead of on paper.
- Day 3: trainers assign one programme each; one diet chart each.
- Day 7: 15-minute call with the owner: what was confusing, what was missing.

## Week 2: run it
- Paper PT registers stop; the app is the record.
- The owner uses the Members tab once and sends WhatsApp messages to Slipping members.
- Day 14: the same call with the owner and both trainers, and the numbers below.

## The numbers (from the Gym screen)
| Number | Good sign |
|---|---|
| Members who check in at least once a week | 70 % or more |
| PT sessions counted in the app vs on paper | all of them by week 2 |
| Flagged PT sessions | few, each settled within 2 days |
| Members with a programme from their trainer | half or more |
| Slipping members messaged on WhatsApp | most of them, and some came back |

## Ask at the end
1. Owner: would you pay for this every month? How much?
2. Trainers: did it save you time? What did you still do on paper or WhatsApp?
3. Members: was check-in quick? Did you use the trainer's routines?
