# Your data at the gym (give this to members)

**Who runs it.** Your gym runs CADENCE on its own server. Your data stays on that server.

**What is kept.** Your profile name, your workouts, body weight and measurements you log, your
check-ins at the desk, your PT sessions, and, if the front desk adds it, your phone number.

**Who sees what.**
- **You** see everything you log.
- **Your trainer**, only if you choose to share with them (Settings → My trainer): your workouts,
  body weight, measurements and the names of your routines. Never your photos, videos or notes.
  They write your diet chart and assign routines; you see both.
- **The owner and the front desk** see your check-ins, your PT sessions, whether you are active,
  your trainer's name, your phone number (to message you on WhatsApp), and in their admin page
  your workouts and body weight. Never your photos, videos or notes.
- **Nobody else.** CADENCE sells nothing and shows no ads.

**Your choices.**
- **Stop sharing** with your trainer any time: they lose access at once. Their private notes
  about you are deleted; you keep their routines and last diet chart.
- **Flag** a PT session that wasn't you, within 48 hours.
- **Download** your data: Settings → Data & backup.
- **Delete** your profile: Settings → **Delete account** (or ask the front desk). Everything goes,
  and cannot come back.

Questions: ask the gym's owner.
